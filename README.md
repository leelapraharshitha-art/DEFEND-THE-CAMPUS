# 🛡️ DEFEND THE CAMPUS — Vignan Campus Outbreak 🧟‍♂️💥

> **Browser Prototype:** Experience the action right in your web browser with zero installation required!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. 📂 Open `index.html` in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. 🚀 Click **`START MISSION`**.
3. 🕹️ **Controls:**
   * **`WASD` / `Arrow Keys`** 🏃💨 Move
   * **`Mouse`** 🎯 Aim
   * **`Left Click`** 🔫 Fire Weapon
   * **`R`** 🔄 Reload
   * **`Enter`** 🔁 Restart (after mission failure)

---

## ✨ Features

* 📍 **Iconic Campus Locations:** Battle through **Library**, **A Block**, **H Block**, **N Block**, **U Block**, and the **Ground** maps.
* 🧟 **Dynamic Zombie Waves:** Escalating survival challenges with continuous enemy spawns.
* 🩸 **Health & Ammo Management:** Monitor your vital stats to survive the outbreak.
* 📦 **Pickups:** Collect health packs 🏥 and ammo crates 📦 scattered across the map.
* 📊 **Campus-Defense HUD:** Real-time stats display for score, health, ammo, and wave counts.
* 🗺️ **Minimap:** Track surrounding threats and navigate campus layouts.
* 🚪 **Area-Clear Transitions:** Secure one zone to unlock progress to the next block.
* 🔄 **Reload System:** Tactical reloading mechanics for intense combat moments.
